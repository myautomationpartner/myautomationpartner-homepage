Facebook requirements

Facebook business Pages are managed by personal Facebook profiles. The personal profile controls access; customers see the business Page.

Important

  • You need a real personal Facebook account to create or manage a business Page.
  • The personal profile is not the business Page and does not replace the business Page.
  • Customers will see the business Page, not the personal profile, when MAP publishes for the business.
  • Do not create a fake personal profile. Facebook may restrict or remove fake accounts.

Confirm before setup

  1. The business has a Facebook Page.
  2. The owner or trusted admin can log in to Facebook.
  3. That person has full control or admin access to the Page.
  4. Security codes and two-factor authentication can be completed during setup.

Instagram and Meta Business Suite

For business use, Instagram should be a professional account and should ideally be connected to your Facebook Page.

Instagram

  • Use an Instagram Business account when possible.
  • If the account is personal, switch it to a professional account before connecting it to MAP.
  • Make sure two-factor authentication is available during setup.

Meta Business Suite

  • Connect the Instagram account to the Facebook Page.
  • Keep the owner or trusted admin as the person who can approve permissions.
  • Add a payment method only if paid ads or boosted posts are part of the plan.

If you are not sure

Do not guess or create duplicate accounts. MAP can help identify the missing step and show you where to continue.

If you do not have accounts yet

These are the basic steps to get ready. You can still start MAP setup before every optional channel is complete.

Create or confirm a real personal Facebook profile.This should belong to the business owner or trusted admin.
Create a Facebook Page for the business.Add business name, profile image, location, website, and contact info.

Website chat and paid ads

These are optional during first setup, but they are useful when your launch plan includes website messages or boosted posts.

Website chat

  • You need access to your website builder, website admin, or developer.
  • MAP will provide a website chat script.
  • The script is usually added before the closing body tag of the website.
  • Website chat should ask visitors for name and email so conversations become real contacts.

Paid ads and boosted posts

  • You only need this if you plan to run paid promotions.
  • Confirm the business has a Meta ad account and current payment method.
  • Decide who can approve budgets.
  • MAP should not launch paid ads without customer approval.

Security best practices

Use these rules for every account before connecting anything to MAP.

Protect access

  • Keep the business owner as the primary admin.
  • Use business-owned email addresses when possible.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.

Keep records clean

  • Keep recovery email and phone numbers current.
  • Remove old employees or vendors who no longer need access.
  • Avoid using one employee’s personal login as the only way into a business account.

Never share passwords

Do not send passwords through email, text, or chat. MAP uses official login and approval screens when a connection is needed.
