Simple social setup guide

Set up Facebook, Instagram, X, and TikTok.

A plain-English guide for creating business social accounts, keeping ownership clean, and avoiding duplicate accounts later.

Quick checklist

  • ✓Owner or trusted admin who can receive security codes.
  • ✓A real personal Facebook profile for the person managing the Facebook Page.

What each account is for

Start with the platforms the business will actually use. It is better to set up four accounts cleanly than to create every possible account with unclear ownership.

Account What it does What you need
Facebook The public business presence customers see on Facebook A real personal Facebook profile plus a business Page
Instagram The public business presence customers see on Instagram A business Instagram account, preferably switched to professional
X Short updates, announcements, public conversation, and handle protection A business-owned login, profile image, bio, and recovery contact
TikTok Short videos, behind-the-scenes content, events, and local visibility A business account name, profile image, bio, and recovery contact

Facebook

Facebook business Pages are managed by real personal Facebook profiles. The profile is the private login; the Page is the public business presence.

Important

  • You need a real personal Facebook account to create or manage a business Page.
  • The personal profile is not the business Page and does not replace the business Page.
  • Customers see the business Page, not the owner’s personal profile, when the business posts.
  • Do not create a fake personal profile. Facebook may restrict or remove fake accounts.

Create or confirm the Page

  1. Log in with the real personal profile that will manage the business.
  2. Create a Page if one does not already exist.
  3. Add business name, logo, website, phone, address or service area, and description.
  4. Add another trusted admin if the business needs backup access.

Instagram

For business use, Instagram should be a professional account and should ideally be connected to your Facebook Page.

Basic setup

  • Create or log in to the business Instagram account.
  • Use the business name or a clear version of it.
  • Add the logo, website, contact info, location or service area, and short bio.
  • Switch the account to a professional account.

Connect it cleanly

  • Choose a business category that fits.
  • Connect Instagram to the Facebook Page in Meta Business Suite when available.
  • Use a business-owned email when possible.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.

X

X is optional for many local businesses, but it can be useful for updates, announcements, public conversation, and protecting the business handle.

Basic setup

  • Create or log in to the business X account.
  • Use the business name or a recognizable handle.
  • Add the logo, website, location, and short bio.

Keep control

  • Use a business-owned email when possible.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Keep the owner or trusted admin as the recovery contact.
  • If you are not ready to post, still consider claiming the right handle.

TikTok

TikTok can be useful for short videos, behind-the-scenes content, events, and local visibility. You can reserve the account now even if you are not ready to post videos yet.

Basic setup

  • Create or log in to the business TikTok account.
  • Use the business name or a clear version of it.
  • Add the logo or recognizable profile image.
  • Add the website or main contact link if available.

Keep control

  • Switch to a business account if that fits the business.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Keep the owner or trusted admin as the recovery contact.

If accounts already exist

Before creating anything new, check what already exists and who controls it.

Look for duplicates

  • Old Facebook Pages with the same or similar business name.
  • Instagram, X, or TikTok accounts created by past employees, contractors, or agencies.
  • Duplicate accounts customers may already be following.

Check ownership

  • Confirm who has admin access or recovery access.
  • Make sure the business owner can receive login codes.
  • Remove people who no longer work with the business.

Recover before replacing

If the right account already exists, try to recover and clean it up before creating another one.

Security best practices

Use these rules for Facebook, Instagram, X, and TikTok.

Protect access

  • Keep the business owner as the primary admin.
  • Use business-owned email addresses when possible.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.

Keep records clean

  • Keep recovery email and phone numbers current.
  • Remove old employees or vendors who no longer need access.
  • Avoid using one employee’s personal login as the only way into a business account.

Never share passwords

Do not send passwords through email, text, or chat. Use official login, invitation, and approval screens whenever possible.

What to have ready before asking for help

If someone is helping you set up accounts, this information will make the process faster and cleaner.

  • ✓X profile link and TikTok profile link if they already exist.
  • ✓Owner/admin email and phone for security codes.
